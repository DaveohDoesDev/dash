Usage Guidelines
Lockup
Website headers, presentations, press kits

Use the full icon + wordmark lockup wherever you have horizontal space. Always maintain the aspect ratio — never stretch or distort.

Icon only
App icons, favicons, social avatars, small badges

The standalone icon works in square or circular containers. Use at 16px minimum for favicons; 32px+ for UI badges; 512px for app stores.

Variants
Dark bg → Dark variant · Light bg → Light variant

Never place the dark variant on a white background or the light variant on dark. Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "D" letterform on all sides.
